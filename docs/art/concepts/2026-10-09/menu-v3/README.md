# Car Stars — regenerated main-menu concepts

Two coordinated portrait menu mockups generated with built-in ImageGen from the original concept references.

- `01-racer-selected.png`: larger Racer scene, gold active role and gold PLAY.
- `02-city-selected.png`: larger city scene, cyan active role and blue PLAY.
- English copy; RACER replaces the former thief role. Both role names and action descriptions sit beneath CHOOSE YOUR SIDE at the bottom.
- Selection uses scene size, sculpted role color and underline/glow, with no written status badge.
- PLAY has no triangle or icon.
- Following user feedback, the cream CHOOSE YOUR SIDE capsule was replaced by small dimensional lettering with navy depth and gold/cyan accents.

Visually inspected both final images for text accuracy, hierarchy, three city patrols, bottom alignment and unclipped controls. These are raster concept references; this request did not replace the editable Figma screens or modify game runtime code.

`prompts.json` preserves the exact generation and refinement prompts. `manifest.json` records source files, dimensions and SHA256 hashes. `verification.json` records file checks.
